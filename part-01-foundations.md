# Part I: Foundations {.unnumbered}

The first part explains how to move from a product idea to a trustworthy experiment.

Chapter 1 starts with the product question and turns it into a decision-ready hypothesis. Chapter 2 shows how that hypothesis anchors the experiment design: treatment, eligibility, exposure, randomization, sample size, and validity checks. Chapter 3 explains how the hypothesis becomes measurable through primary metrics, secondary metrics, guardrails, proxy metrics, ratio metrics, and decomposition.

The goal of this part is to build the basic discipline of experimentation: know what you are testing, design the comparison carefully, and measure the result with metrics that match the decision.
