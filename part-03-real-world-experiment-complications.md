# Part III: Real-World Experiment Complications {.unnumbered}

The third part discusses complications that appear in real product systems.

The chapters follow a simple arc from within-unit exposure problems to across-unit spillover problems:

- Chapter 8 studies within-unit exposure problems: assignment, exposure, and actual treatment received are not always the same thing.
- Chapter 9 studies across-unit spillover problems: one unit's treatment can affect another unit's outcome.

The goal of this part is to move beyond the clean textbook experiment. Real users, marketplaces, recommendations, and product systems create complications that must be handled in the design and interpretation.
