# Part IV: Learning and Optimization {.unnumbered}

The fourth part moves beyond a single average treatment effect.

Chapter 10 introduces heterogeneous treatment effects and uplift modeling, which ask who benefits most or least. Chapter 11 discusses bandits and adaptive experiments, where traffic allocation changes while learning. Chapter 12 covers ranking experiments and interleaving, where the goal is often to compare ranking systems faster and more sensitively than a standard A/B test.

The goal of this part is to show how experimentation can support learning, targeting, ranking, and optimization, not only binary launch decisions.
