# Part V: Experiment Platforms and Operations {.unnumbered}

The fifth part focuses on the operating system around experimentation.

Chapter 13 discusses concurrent experiments and experiment layers, where many tests run at the same time. Chapter 14 covers experiment quality checks, such as sample ratio mismatch, logging correctness, and balance checks. Chapter 15 turns results into decisions, including practical significance, guardrail tradeoffs, rollout strategy, and decision-making under uncertainty.

The goal of this part is to connect statistical experiments with production practice. A good experimentation culture needs platforms, checks, review habits, and decision rules, not only p-values.
